## Why A4 is the usual choice

Every ISO A size is the same shape — halve the long side and you get the next
size down — so a drawing measured for A4 can be reprinted at A3 or A5 without
recomposing it. Only the numbers change. That is not true of the US sizes,
which is worth knowing before committing a stencil to Letter.

## The closest alternative

US Letter is 215.9 × 279.4 mm: fractionally wider than A4 and noticeably
shorter. A layout that just fits A4 will usually lose something off the bottom
on Letter, so pick one at the start rather than converting later.

## Margins

Most printers cannot reach the edge of the sheet. Keep critical marks 5–10 mm
inside the trim, or print borderless if your printer supports it.
